YOUYOU V8.9 — STRIPE PAYMENT LINK

What changed
- Landing Studio now supports merchant-owned Stripe Payment Links.
- Choose Stripe payment link in Conversion & payment, paste a buy.stripe.com URL and test it.
- Keep Lead form ON to capture the request before payment, or turn it OFF for direct checkout.

Before going live
- Create and test the link in a Stripe Sandbox first.
- Confirm that product, price and currency match between YOUYOU and Stripe.
- Replace the Sandbox link with the merchant's live Stripe Payment Link only after testing.

No SQL migration and no Stripe secret/API key are required for this version.
See V8.9-STRIPE-PAYMENT-LINK-AUDIT.txt for the full audit and test checklist.
