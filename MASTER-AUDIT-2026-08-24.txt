HIGH PRIORITY FINDINGS
1. SEO desktop had several layers of CSS overrides from V4.13/V4.14/V4.15 and older rules. These were competing at normal browser zoom and caused oversized hero/configuration. Added a final compact/readable override rather than changing SEO logic.
2. AI Studio route exists and renders correctly in this base. UI text was unusually small (many 6–8px labels). Increased desktop readability without changing generation logic.
3. Landing Page Builder dedicated routes exist and are healthy:
   /dashboard/landing-pages/new?template=...
   /dashboard/landing-pages/edit/:id
4. Landing template presets used many dark palettes and the gallery shared nearly identical mini layouts. Added light/pastel palettes to multiple presets, automatic contrast text, and layout-specific visual geometry.
5. Public marketing site was English-only. Added EN / FR / AR selector, persisted in localStorage, with RTL for Arabic and translations for the major acquisition sections.
6. Public homepage did not showcase Smart Landing Pages despite it becoming a core product pillar. Added a dedicated visual section.
