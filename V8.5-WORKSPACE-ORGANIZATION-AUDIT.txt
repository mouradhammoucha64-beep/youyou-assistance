YOUYOU V8.5 — WORKSPACE ORGANIZATION AUDIT

Source audited: user-provided full project ZIP (19)(1), renderer 8.4.0.

Root causes / findings:
1) Workspace controls were technically grouped, but the visual grouping was only a small uppercase heading + divider. With 15 editable sections this becomes a long scanning problem.
2) Business email was always visible even though it is optional.
3) Visitor email was always rendered in the public lead form, even when the business did not need it.
4) Important bug found in the exported/public-page inline JavaScript: email regexes inside a template literal used \s without double escaping. The generated HTML could therefore degrade \s to s and reject otherwise valid email addresses containing the letter s.

Changes in V8.5:
- 15 workspace sections converted into numbered premium collapsible cards.
- One active section at a time; last open section remembered per landing-page draft for the session.
- Each card has a live status chip (price, gallery count, product options ON/OFF, etc.).
- Business email is hidden when empty and appears only through + Add business email; existing saved email remains visible.
- Removing business email clears it cleanly and autosaves.
- Choosing CTA=Email automatically reveals the business email input when needed.
- New Visitor email field control under Conversion: Hidden by default / Show optional email.
- Public lead form omits the visitor email field unless enabled.
- Fixed the exported/public runtime email-regex escaping bug.
- New pages no longer auto-copy the company business email into every landing page.

No database schema change. No SQL required.
