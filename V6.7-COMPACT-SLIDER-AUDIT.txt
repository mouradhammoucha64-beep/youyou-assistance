YOUYOU V6.7 — COMPACT SLIDER

- Based on V6.6.
- Beauty Product defaults remain Static Images, not Slider.
- Slider redesigned as compact carousel: 3 cards desktop, 2 tablet, 82% card mobile with next-card peek.
- Mobile slider height reduced to 176px (164px on very small screens).
- Desktop slider height reduced to 250px.
- Arrows hidden on mobile in favor of native swipe.
- Dots moved below media rather than overlaying images.
- Arrow/autoplay scrolling now advances one card instead of one full viewport.
